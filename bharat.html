An interactive map of ~600 million Indian jobs across 43 representative occupations. Each tile's area = number of workers; recolour it by AI exposure, wages, growth, education, informality or gender. India's labour market is unlike most others — ~85% of work is informal and agriculture still employs the largest share.

This is a thinking tool, not an economic forecast. Employment counts are calibrated to the macro shares reported in India's Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS 2023-24) — agriculture ~45%, services ~29%, construction ~13%, manufacturing ~11% — and then distributed across 43 representative occupations. AI-exposure scores are model estimates of how much today's AI could reshape a role (automation + augmentation), not predictions of job loss. A high score means the work changes a lot, not that the job disappears — demand, regulation, cost and the vast informal/physical economy all push the other way. Salaries are indicative median monthly figures (₹) and vary hugely by city and formality.
